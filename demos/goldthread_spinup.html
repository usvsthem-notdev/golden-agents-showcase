Cat Gossip

Core Problem — Cat owners have nowhere dedicated to gossip about their cats: who's beefing with the tabby next door, who got into the neighbor's koi pond again. General social apps bury pet content in a feed built for humans. Cat Gossip exists to give cat drama a home.

  • A user can post a cat update with a photo and an optional location tag
  • The feed ranks by gossip heat (reaction velocity), not just recency
  • Report and block ship before any public launch
